01 / The lab

Slope is not a formula. It’s a climb.

Drag the sliders. Watch the line. The blue number is how steep the climb is. The black number is where the line begins. That’s all y = mx + b ever was.

03 / The method

Heuristic, from the Greek: to find.

We don’t ask kids to memorize a procedure they can’t see. We give them a way to find the idea — then the procedure writes itself.

01

Draw the quantity

A variable is a changing pile, a length, a height. Not a letter hunting for a number.

02

Move it

Slope, balance, area, throw-paths. If it can’t be moved, it isn’t ready to be named.

03

Name it last

Once the picture is theirs, x and y become nicknames. Algebra stops being a foreign language.
